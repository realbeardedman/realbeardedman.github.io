Privacy policy for the “Sweet Spot” app

Android app “Sweet Spot: Dial-in Brew Coach” (package app.sweetspot.dialin) · Last updated: November 2026

In short: Sweet Spot has no accounts and no server of its own. Everything you enter stays on your device. We do not receive any personal data from you.

2. Data on your device

The app stores beans, setups (grinder, machine, water), recipes, ratings, notes, the log and completed care tasks only locally on your device. This data is not transferred to us or to third parties. If you uninstall the app or use “Delete all data” in the settings, it is removed.

Android can back up app data through your Google account’s backup feature (Auto Backup) if you have turned this on for your device. Google and you control this backup through the Android settings; we have no access to it.

3. Location

Only when you tap “Nearest city by location” under tap water does the app ask for your approximate location. It uses it once, on the device, to find the nearest city in its list. The location is neither stored nor transferred. The app does not request your precise location. You can revoke the permission at any time in the Android settings.

4. Camera and photos

When adding beans, you can take a photo of the coffee bag or pick an existing photo. The app then reads the name, roaster, origin, process, roast level and roast date from the label and fills them into the form, where you check them. The app asks for camera permission only when you take a photo; to pick a photo it uses the Android photo picker and receives only the selected image, with no access to your gallery.

Text recognition runs on your device using Google ML Kit, which is included in the app. The photo is not transferred and is deleted from the app’s cache after recognition. Only the details you save in the form are stored.

According to Google, ML Kit sends technical diagnostic and usage data to Google in encrypted form during text recognition, such as device manufacturer and model, Android version, package name and app version, performance metrics such as recognition time, error codes and a per-installation identifier. According to Google, the photo and the recognized text are not part of this data. Google uses it to operate and improve ML Kit and, by its own account, does not share it with third parties. Details: ML Kit data disclosure and Google Privacy Policy.

5. Backup as a file

Under “Settings → Backup” you can export your data as a file. You decide where the file goes (for example Google Drive, email or Files) through the Android share menu. The terms of the respective provider apply to the storage you choose.

6. Links to other websites

The app contains links, for example to sources of the catalog data, to water suppliers and to products on Amazon. When you tap one, the page opens in your browser. Only then does the respective provider process data such as your IP address under its own privacy policy.

Amazon Associates program: As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases. Links to Amazon contain an associate tag that Amazon uses to attribute a purchase to the app; Amazon may set cookies for this. The app itself transfers nothing except the link you open. We receive no data about you from Amazon, only a statement of commissions. Amazon’s privacy notice is linked at the bottom of every page on amazon.de.

7. Advertising

The app shows product tips with Amazon affiliate links, matched to the topic of the page and labeled “Ad”, as well as a buying guide with links to equipment and accessories. They are selected only on the device based on the content (for example a note on water hardness), without a user profile. Premium removes them; the buying guide and its links remain.

The app does not currently show ads from ad networks. If that changes, you will be asked for your consent in the app beforehand, and this policy will be updated accordingly.

8. Premium (in-app purchase)

Premium removes the ads. You buy it through Google Play with your Google account; Google handles payment and invoicing under its own privacy policy. We receive no payment details and no information about you, only a statement of sales from Google. At startup the app asks Google Play on your device whether the purchase exists and remembers the result locally.

9. No analytics of our own, no tracking

The app contains no analytics or tracking tools of its own and does not build user profiles. For the diagnostic data Google ML Kit sends when reading a bag, see section 4.

10. Your rights

Since we do not receive any personal data from you, there is generally nothing we could provide information about or delete. Regardless, under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the Bavarian State Office for Data Protection Supervision (BayLDA).

11. Changes

If the app changes so that other data is processed, we will update this policy. The version published here applies. If the English and German versions differ, the German version prevails.
